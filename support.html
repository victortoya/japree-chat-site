How to Use Japree

  1. Fill In Your Profile — Open the Profile menu and enter your full name, Student ID (NIM), and department or faculty. This information will be automatically included in every message you compose.
  2. Create a New Message — Tap the add (+) button on the home screen to start composing a message.
  3. Select Message Components — Choose an opening greeting, time-of-day salutation, purpose of your message, your question or request, and a closing phrase.
  4. Preview Your Message — Review the fully composed message. You can edit the text directly if needed.
  5. Send or Save — Tap Send to share the message via WhatsApp, SMS, or any other app using the iOS Share Sheet. Or tap Save to store it for future use.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is my data safe?

Yes. All data — including your profile and message history — is stored only on your device and is never sent to our servers. Japree works entirely offline.

Why do I need to fill in my profile first?

Your name, Student ID, and department are automatically included in every message you compose. Without a complete profile, the app cannot generate a message.

Are my messages saved automatically?

No. After composing a message, you need to tap Save on the Preview screen and enter a title for the message. Saved messages can be viewed and reused from the home screen.

How do I edit a saved message?

Tap the message on the home screen to open it in the Preview screen. Edit the text directly in the message area, then tap Save to update it.

How do I delete a message?

On the home screen, swipe left on the message item and tap Delete, or tap the delete icon. Confirm the deletion in the dialog that appears. Deleted messages cannot be recovered.

Will my data transfer if I switch to a new iPhone?

No. Because all data is stored locally on your device, it will not transfer to a new device automatically. Be sure to note any important messages before switching devices.

Why is the app asking for ad tracking permission?

Japree displays ads from Google AdMob to support ongoing app development. The permission prompt is part of Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) framework. You may decline personalized ads — the app will continue to work fully regardless of your choice.

Is Japree available on Android?

Japree is currently available on iOS only. We will announce any future Android availability through the App Store.
