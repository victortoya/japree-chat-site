Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 9, 2026 Last updated: October 9, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Japree, an iOS application that helps Indonesian students compose formal, polite messages to their lecturers or teachers. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, store, and use information when you use the Japree app.

By using Japree, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please discontinue use of the application.

2. Information We Collect

Japree distinguishes between two types of data: information you enter yourself (stored only on your device) and data collected by third-party advertising services.

2.1 User Profile Data On-device only

When you fill in your profile, the app stores the following data locally on your device using UserDefaults:

Data Description Storage
Full name Your name, included automatically in every composed message Local (device)
Student ID (NIM) Your academic identifier, included in every composed message Local (device)
Department / Faculty Your study program or faculty, included in every composed message Local (device)

2.2 Saved Messages On-device only

Messages you compose and save are stored locally on your device using CoreData. This includes: the message title, message body, and creation date.

No personal data is ever sent to our servers. Japree operates entirely offline. All profile data and message history remain exclusively on your device and never leave it.

3. Third-Party Advertising Services

Japree displays ads from Google AdMob (Google LLC) to support the development of this app. As part of its advertising service, Google may collect certain data from your device, including:

  • Device identifiers (IDFA / IDFV)
  • App usage data for serving relevant ads
  • IP address

Google's collection and use of data is governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

In compliance with applicable regulations (GDPR / CCPA / ATT), Japree uses the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request your consent before displaying personalized ads.

4. How We Use Your Information

The profile data and message history stored on your device are used solely to:

  • Auto-fill your identity (name, student ID, department) in every composed message
  • Save messages you have composed so they can be reused later

We do not use your data for analytics, tracking, or any other commercial purpose.

5. Data Storage & Security

All user data (profile and message history) is stored exclusively on your device. We do not operate any server or cloud database to store your information.

The security of your data depends on the security of your own device. We recommend enabling a passcode or Face ID / Touch ID on your iOS device.

All data is automatically deleted when you uninstall the Japree app from your device.

6. Your Rights & Controls

Because all data is stored locally on your device, you have full control over it at all times:

  • Edit or delete profile: Update or clear your profile data at any time via the Profile menu in the app.
  • Delete saved messages: Remove any saved message at any time from the Message List screen.
  • Delete all data: Uninstalling the app removes all data from your device permanently.

For questions about data collected by Google AdMob, please visit Google Ad Center to manage your ad preferences.

7. Children's Privacy

Japree is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you become aware that a child under 13 is using this app, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be communicated via an app update on the App Store. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect the most recent revision.

Your continued use of the app after changes take effect constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
